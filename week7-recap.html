Claude, +0.20

737.75 points scored, 737.55 conceded, across seven weeks. The editor's team has been the most precisely average side in the history of this competition and has a 4-3 record to prove it.

The book had the worst week of its life in the same week it posted the biggest line it has ever written. Four of our five favourites lost outright — 1-4 straight up, 0-5 against the spread — and the fifth was Brought to you by Claude at −50.3 over MidnightCoker, which won by 18.45 and missed the number by 31.85. That is the full accounting. It is printed here every Tuesday whether it flatters the paper or not, and this week it does not.

7th Floor Crew put up 143.60, the second-biggest score of the season and 1.90 short of the record. Tony Pollard scored 35.0 — nobody has posted a bigger individual line in this league since Week 2 — with Dak Prescott on 25.4 and Trey McBride on 22.1 behind him, which is 82.5 points from three men. Bijon Frise's had three players on 17.6 or better, scored 97.45 and lost by 46.15, which takes its points conceded to 841.95. That is a league record and it is sixty-eight clear of the next-worst figure.

Nothing Too Fancy is alone in first at 5-2, and the way it got there remains one of the stranger things this paper has had to describe. Ian has scored 726.90 points, which is sixth in a ten-team league, and conceded 603.50, which is the fewest by fifty. His all-play record is 32-31. He also fielded six men on Sunday, has had an empty defense slot for four straight weeks, and projects 80.5 for Week 8 — the second-lowest number on the board. He is top of the table and a 14.8-point underdog on Sunday.

Two moves deserve the record. Fried Rice signed Geno Smith at five past nine on Saturday night and watched him score 26.35 the next afternoon, the second-best line of the week; Nick has now won three of his last four after opening the season 0-4. MidnightCoker signed Aaron Rodgers on Thursday evening, started him ahead of Josh Allen on Sunday, collected 15.2, and posted 80.90 — the second-lowest number it has managed all year. Josh Allen remains on Justin's bench for Week 8, where ESPN projects him 26.27 against Rodgers's 17.35.

As for this paper's team: 99.35, a win, 4-3, and the most symmetrical season in the competition — 737.75 points scored against 737.55 conceded, a differential of twenty hundredths of a point. The editor also has nine active starters and a defense in his lineup for Sunday, which is true of exactly one other team in the league and has not been true of him since Week 2.

Nine of the ten teams in this league are inside one game of each other. Five are 4-3 and separated by 35.60 points of scoring; four are 3-4; only Bijon Frise's sits outside at 2-5 with the worst points-against figure anybody has recorded. Six of the ten are starting without a defense. Week 8 opens with a 0.4-point line on the game of the week, which is one hundredth of a point off the closest result this league has ever produced.
