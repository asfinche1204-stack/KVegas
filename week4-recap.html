Correspondence
Grievance upheld

The paper described Ja'Marr Chase's 4.2 as a healthy receiver playing football on purpose, and was wrong; Fried Rice lost three early picks in the first half. The 59.35 is recorded with an asterisk. The low-score record survived by 4.30.

Bijon Frise's won a game. At 8 o'clock on Sunday it sat at 71.70, trailing Tiger King Dethroned by 39.2, and this paper wrote that Puka Nacua's 23.2 had "come too late to matter." Then Tetairoa McMillan scored 38.2 and Bryce Young 24.75 — 62.95 points after the read, the second-biggest late rally of the season — and Michael beat the team with the most points in the league 134.65 to 123.00. He is 1-3 with 469.55 points against, the most anyone has absorbed, and he has now been the subject of four straight lineup checks. This week's entry reads: clean.

Country Roads Take Mahomes is first. CeeDee Lamb went 32.8, Nico Collins 27.3 and Tee Higgins 21.2 for 135.55, the best number of the week, and the editor's team finished at 68.65 — the lowest score anyone has posted since Week 1 — in a game this paper had promoted to Game of the Week on the grounds that Brought to you by Claude led the league in points. It no longer does. Tiger King does, at 460.45, while sitting 2-2, having lost by 0.40 and 11.65 and having not started a defense since the season began.

Fried Rice is 0-4, and the record book survived by four and a bit. Nick sat at 33.95 at the read with one starter to come; Chuba Hubbard scored 25.4 and the final was 59.35, which leaves Gonna Nacau You's 55.05 as the lowest score in league history. The paper notes, having been corrected in writing on Sunday night, that Fried Rice lost its first, second and fourth-round picks inside the first half, that its lineup has been cleared by the lineup check every single week, and that its luck index of −1.11 remains the worst in the league. The 59.35 goes in the book with an asterisk.

The one live game on Sunday night flipped the way the projection said it would. Gonna Nacau You trailed Nothing Too Fancy at the read, finished at 109.05 to Ian's 98.70, and is 3-1 — with a luck index of +1.22, the highest in the league, on a 16-20 all-play record. Ian is also 3-1 and now has the second-best points in the league behind two 2-2 teams. And Lil' Tuten has scored 109.65 in consecutive weeks since claiming C.J. Stroud off waivers, which is the quietest turnaround of the season.

One league-wide note for the ledger: three of the ten teams are now playing without a defense. Tiger King has gone four weeks, 7th Floor four, and MidnightCoker dropped the Browns D/ST on Thursday and did not replace it. Between them they are 6-6. The paper has no editorial position on this, beyond observing that ESPN keeps counting eight.
