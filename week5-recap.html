The trade
Harvey for Black

First trade in this league's season, processed by the commissioner at 12:10 on Friday: Zachubus sent RJ Harvey to Nothing Too Fancy for Kaelon Black. Zachubus dropped Kaelon Black at 1:35 the following afternoon.

Brought to you by Claude was 24.40 behind at Sunday night's read with four starters to play against one, and ESPN had it winning by 2.2. It won by 13.45. Matthew Stafford went 31.15, Bucky Irving 30.5 and Derrick Henry 20.5, and 75.70 became 127.35 — a post-read move of 51.65, the third-largest this paper has recorded in this league. 7th Floor Crew's 113.90 was the best score anybody lost with all week.

Fried Rice won a game. Nick finished on Sunday afternoon with 135.95 — the best number in the league this week — and watched Bijon Frise's need 54.1 points from two starters to catch him. Michael got 29.2 from Puka Nacua and finished on 111.05, which took his points conceded to 605.50 in five weeks. That is a league record, it is thirty-one and a half clear of the next-worst figure, and it is why a team averaging 95.63 a week with Jayden Daniels and Puka Nacua on it is 1-4.

The 0.20-point game turned into a ten-point one. MidnightCoker beat Country Roads Take Mahomes 103.95 to 93.85 off 67.8 points from Jahmyr Gibbs and Josh Allen, which moved Justin to second and gave him 557.00 season points — the most in the league, by four tenths of a point over the editor. Patrick, who was in first place a week ago, is fourth. Nothing Too Fancy is top on 548.40 and 422.75 against, the fewest anybody has conceded by a margin of twelve.

Zachubus had the week the name suggests. Renamed at 12:09 on Friday, a minute before completing the first trade in league history — RJ Harvey out, Kaelon Black in — it then dropped Kaelon Black at half past one on Saturday afternoon, and lost 134.55 to 76.00 on Sunday. The team is 3-2. It is also 17-28 against the field, ninth in the league in points, and last in this week's power rankings, which is a combination this paper has not had to print before.

And then there is Sunday. Nothing Too Fancy is 4-1 and in first place, and ESPN projects it to score 52.2 — five starters active, Joe Burrow unable to play, and an empty defense slot where the Seahawks were until 1:07 on Sunday afternoon. Fried Rice, 1-4, projects 61.1 and is an 8.9-point favourite over first place. Country Roads fields six and projects 64.0 with Jalen Hurts on the bench. The only complete nine-man lineup in the league belongs to Bijon Frise's, who are 1-4 and favoured by 36.7, the biggest line this paper has posted. Four teams will start without a defense. It is going to be an ugly, ugly week, and the standings are going to look very strange on Tuesday.
