The bag
Tiger King, +19.3

Biggest beat of the Sunday-night projection: 114.4 projected, 133.70 final. Ryan went the other way, 75.9 projected, 55.05 final, −20.9.

Kenneth Walker III scored 32.6 on Monday night and 7th Floor Crew went from a 2.3-point lead to a 19.55-point loss. Zach had one player left. Joe had three. Joe's three added 10.75; Zach's one added 32.6. Tiger King Dethroned is 1-0, Joe is 0-1 with the fifth-best score in the league, and Sunday's swing meter, which had Joe by five, has been asked to clean out its desk.

Everywhere else the read held. Secret Achane Man finished at 145.5 for the high score of the week — Jaxson Dart doubled his Sunday-night line to 28.9 in the second half, with Swift's 31.9 and Jefferson's 27.2 already in the bank. Nothing Too Fancy's Monday player added 3.6, and that was enough: Ian finished at 135.15, 1.1 clear of Brought to you by Claude's 134.05, which was the top of the board at 7:30 Sunday and third by Tuesday morning. The editor has been informed and is handling it well.

Country Roads got 14.1 from Cam Skattebo on Sunday night and beat Fried Rice 97.65 to 80.65, which means Nick's 39.95 from Caleb Williams goes down as the best individual score in the league, in a loss. Rashee Rice's 8.9 on Monday was the rest of the week. Gonna Nacau You finished at 55.05 — Kyren Williams 14.0, McCaffrey 11.3, two Monday players for 7.45 combined — and would have lost to every other team by at least 25. The good news for Ryan is that Justin is up next, and Justin's roster scored 30.8 outside of Allen and Gibbs.

Two things from the tables. The all-play record says Joe was the unluckiest owner in Week 1: five wins against the field, zero in the standings. Patrick was the luckiest: three wins against the field, 1-0. File it away for when one of them starts talking about the schedule. And the family standings open at Lingafelts 1-1, Zimmermans 1-1, McNamaras 1-2 — Patrick is the only McNamara who won, and he did it with the seventh-best score.
