Five league records fell on Sunday and the team at the top of the table set two of the wrong ones. MidnightCoker scored 154.90, the highest score this league has recorded, one week after scoring 63.90, its lowest of the season. Christian McCaffrey scored 44.3 for Zachubus, the biggest individual line in league history by a fifth of a point. And Now I'll Do A Lil' Tuten went top of the league by winning 72.90 to 56.10: the lowest winning score of the year, in the lowest-scoring game ever played here.

Take Justin first. Our book made MidnightCoker a 3.2-point favourite over first place last Tuesday, off a 63.90 and with a receiver on IR in his lineup, and this page said it stood by the number without sounding as if it meant it. He scored 154.90. Jahmyr Gibbs had 40.7, Deebo Samuel 24.4, and Emmett Johnson, signed at 8:43 on Saturday night in exchange for Aaron Rodgers, had 32.1. Nothing Too Fancy scored 120.15, which beats most teams in most weeks, and lost by 34.75 in the highest-scoring game the league has seen.

That result handed first place to Jordan, who took it with 72.90. He has the most points in the league, the fewest conceded and the best all-play record, and had spent the previous fortnight looking up at somebody else all the same. On Sunday he went 1-8 against the field and won, because Bijon Frise's scored 56.10, the second-lowest total in league history, with Bryce Young at quarterback ahead of Jayden Daniels. Young scored 10.1. He starts again this week.

Country Roads beat Fried Rice 126.65 to 124.90, a margin of 1.75 and the second-closest game of the season. Nick has now scored more points than he has conceded and is 4-6. He had Saquon Barkley on his bench when this page checked last Tuesday and has him there again this morning, and at some point those stop being two separate observations.

7th Floor Crew scored 138.65 as a 19.2-point underdog, with 75.85 of it from George Pickens and Dak Prescott, and goes top of our power rankings at 6-4. Tiger King lost that game and has now conceded 1,123.00 points, more than anybody. And the editor, an 8.2-point favourite over the team with the worst all-play record in the league, scored 79.90 and lost by 24.10 to a running back.

Two teams are 7-3, one is 6-4, three are 5-5 and three are 4-6. Four weeks remain, six of ten make the playoffs, and every team in the league is still within two games of a place.
