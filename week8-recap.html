The quiet week
8 moves

Eight transactions in eight days, the fewest this season, and six of the ten teams made none at all. Four of the eight belong to Nothing Too Fancy, who are top of the table and have had an empty defense slot since October 11.

Joe Burrow scored 44.10 points on Sunday, which is more than anybody has scored in a single week in this league's history. It beats Josh Allen's 43.30 from Week 2, it arrived alongside 24.7 from Brock Bowers in a 130.40, and it put Nothing Too Fancy clear at the top of the table at 6-2. Ian has now scored 857.30 points — third in a ten-team league — and conceded 715.05, the fewest anybody has by nineteen. He has also not fielded a defense since the eleventh of October and projects to field eight men again on Sunday.

At the other end, Tiger King Dethroned scored 56.60. That is the second-lowest number this competition has recorded, 1.55 above Zachubus's 55.05 from the opening Sunday, and it came with Harold Fannin Jr. leading the team on 18.4 and the D/ST slot empty for a ninth consecutive week. Fried Rice won that game by 78.90 — one tenth of a point short of the biggest margin of victory this league has ever produced, a record held by this paper's own team since Week 1. Zach is still 4-4.

Now I'll Do A Lil' Tuten had the best score of the week at 143.20 and now has 916.55 for the season, the most anybody has held at any stage. Bhayshul Tuten 28.9, D'Andre Swift 26.9 and Jordan Love 24.4, off a 0.4-point line — the tightest this paper has posted — which it won by 37.55. Josh Allen returned to MidnightCoker's starting lineup after a fortnight on the bench and scored 29.45, which settles the argument this column has been running with Justin since the twenty-second of October and did absolutely nothing about the result.

Country Roads got 63.15 from two men, Tee Higgins on 32.5 and Kirk Cousins on 30.65. Cousins was signed on October 17 at the cost of the Panthers defense, and he has now outscored every other quarterback on that roster for two weeks running while Jared Goff sits on the bench projecting better than him. These two facts are both true and this paper has stopped trying to reconcile them.

As for the editor: 115.55 and a 2.70 win, which is not what a 19.2-point favourite is supposed to look like. Drake Maye 24.45, Bucky Irving 21.0, Smith-Njigba 20.8, and Bijon Frise's answered with three men over 19.5 and lost anyway — his seventh defeat, on 957.50 points conceded, which is a league record and seventy-six clear of the next-worst. Michael is 2-6 and has the only complete nine-man lineup in the league besides Zachubus.

The league scored 1,101.50 on Sunday, the biggest week of the year. One team is 6-2, two are 5-3, four are 4-4, two are 3-5 and one is 2-6, and six weeks remain. On Sunday the editor fields seven men and 90.0 against first place's 114.5, which our book makes a 24.5-point line — the largest on the board and a number this paper would rather not have had to write.
