The silence
3 moves

Three transactions in eight days, the quietest week this league has had, after a Week 8 that set the previous record at eight. Seven of the ten teams did nothing at all. Six of them are still starting eight men because nobody has a defense.

Now I'll Do A Lil' Tuten has scored a thousand points. 1,051.55, to be exact, which no team in this league has reached at any stage, and it arrived on a Sunday when Jordan Love scored 39.9, Matthew Golden 27.4 and Christian Watson 26.6 — 93.90 of a 135.00 from three men in the same offense. Jordan has the most points in the competition, the best all-play record in it at 56-25, and a 6-3 record that leaves him a game behind the team in first place. That is the whole league in one line.

The team in first place is Nothing Too Fancy, 7-2 and two clear, having beaten this paper's own team 122.25 to 98.00. Our book made that a 24.5-point line; it was decided by 24.25, so the editor lost the game and covered the spread by a quarter of a point. Ian has conceded 813.05 points, fewest in the league by 36.80, a 47-34 all-play that says the fixture list is doing about a game and three quarters of the work, and no defense in the D/ST slot since the eleventh of October. He projects 95.2 for Sunday and is an underdog in our own book.

At the other end, two numbers. MidnightCoker scored 63.90, the lowest Justin has managed all season and the fifth-lowest in this league's history, with Josh Allen leading the team on 15.2. And Tiger King Dethroned, which scored 56.60 seven days ago, scored 113.00 — the only team in the league that improved on Week 8 at all, and it did so by 56.40. Zach is 5-4 and fifth, with ten straight Sundays and no defense.

Fried Rice went the other way: 135.50 and a 78.90-point win became 86.05 and a 48.95-point defeat, a drop of 49.45 that is the largest week-to-week fall on this page. Nick signed Devin Singletary at 9:57 on Sunday morning and started him at running back with Saquon Barkley on the bench, which is the single most consequential decision anybody in this league made last week and it was made by somebody who is 4-5.

Bijon Frise's won by 29.90 off Puka Nacua's 28.0 and Jayden Daniels's 17.3 and has both of them on the bench for Sunday. Michael is 3-6 with 1,021.40 points conceded, a league record and 34.90 clear of anybody else, and a 34-47 all-play that says he is better than his record by most of a game. 7th Floor Crew beat Zachubus 105.15 to 79.00 and is 5-4 and third, with Tony Pollard back on the bench and the lowest projection on the board for Sunday.

The league projected 1,002.4 for Week 9 and scored 999.55, which is the closest this page's number has come all year and will not be mentioned again until it misses. Three transactions in eight days, seven teams doing nothing at all, six of the ten still without a defense, five weeks left, and every single team in the competition inside two games of a playoff place.
