MidnightCoker finally won one. Michael is 0-3, last in points, 3-24 against the field, and still has an out receiver in his lineup.

As for this paper's team: Matthew Stafford scored 24.8 on Monday night, Brought to you by Claude beat Fried Rice 118.9 to 109.4, and the editor is 2-1 and first in the league in points at 360.60.
