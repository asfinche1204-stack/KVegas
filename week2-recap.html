Then the Game of the Week. Brought to you by Claude led Tiger King Dethroned 77.40 to 60.05 at the read; Zach had Jonathan Taylor and Kenneth Walker III on Sunday night, and they scored 27.2 and 20.8 — 48.0 — while Matthew Stafford answered with 30.25 on Monday. Smith-Njigba, Stafford and Derrick Henry were 84.45 of the editor's 107.65, and the other six starters combined for 23.2. The two teams are now 0.05 apart in season points. The editor would like it known that the editor is fine.

Country Roads went to 2-0 behind CeeDee Lamb's 31.3 and beat Now I'll Do A Lil' Tuten 122.5 to 68.5, the largest margin of the week; Jordan's Monday pair, Dart and Nabers, added 1.6. The team that scored 145.5 in Week 1 as Secret Achane Man scored 68.5 in Week 2 under its new name, and this paper is drawing no conclusions. Nothing Too Fancy beat Bijon Frise's 108.95 to 63.8 and leads the league in record and points, 244.1; Michael's two Monday players, Nacua and the Rams D/ST, added 0.0, which is exactly what ESPN projected on Sunday. And 7th Floor Crew beat Fried Rice 88.75 to 77.6 — the lowest winning score of the season, from the other team in the league without a defense.

From the tables: Patrick has the fewest points against in the league, 149.15, and leads the luck index at +0.89, 2-0 on a 10-8 record against the field.
